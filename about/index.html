About Ezra John Gubatanga

A look into my journey of building brands, scaling paid traffic campaigns, and developing modern digital products.

I have spent the past 8+ years working at the intersection of digital marketing, creative content strategy, and web development. Unlike traditional specialists who operate in silos, I look at the entire digital growth ecosystem as a single machine.

My career began in the fast-paced world of brand management, where I managed channels for multiple consumer retail and food brands simultaneously. This environment taught me how to coordinate campaign events, create high-impact viral content structures, and structure meta advertising strategies that scale.

As I scaled brands, I quickly realized that driving traffic is only half the battle. If a website loads slowly or offers a frustrating mobile experience, even the best paid ad campaigns will bleed revenue. To bridge this gap, I dove deep into web development—mastering clean semantics, database structures, and performance optimizations.

Today, I build web solutions using modern environments like Supabase, Vercel, and Resend, and use tools like Claude Code and Cursor to design conversion-optimized products. My goal is to build experiences that look premium, perform flawlessly, and scale businesses efficiently.
